Merge two or more business profiles

If your business has several domains, you may have multiple Trustpilot profiles. Merging them into one makes it easier for customers to find you and helps build trust. Managing your reviews and TrustScore is also much simpler when everything is in one place. You can request a merge if your business qualifies for one of the three reasons below.

Reasons for merging

Identical domains

You have multiple domains that redirect to one main domain, share identical content, or contain the same content in different languages.

Rebranding

You've changed your business name or brand, and your old domain now redirects to your new one.

Business merger or change of ownership

Your business has acquired, merged with, or taken over one or more other businesses, and all entities now operate as a single brand.

Considerations before merging profiles

Profile merges are permanent and cannot be reversed. Before submitting a request, please review our Guidelines for merging business profiles.

Important: If you're an existing Trustpilot Business customer planning to add a new domain to your current subscription, do not register your new domain on Trustpilot yourself. Doing so may cause billing and account routing issues. Please contact your Account Manager first.

Prepare your accounts

Complete the steps below to prepare your Trustpilot Business accounts for the merge.

Claim your main business profile

Make sure your main Trustpilot profile is claimed. If your additional domain hasn't been created on Trustpilot yet, or you haven't claimed it, don't worry — our Support team will help create and connect it to the correct account as part of the merge process.

Redirect your domains

All domains included in the merge must automatically redirect to the main domain. An exception applies if the domains have identical content.

Check your Admin permissions

You must be an Admin for any currently claimed domains involved in this merge. If your additional domain hasn't been created yet, Admin access for your main profile is sufficient. The email address used for this request must match your Admin email exactly, or we won't be able to process it. See roles and permissions here.

Confirm domains are on the same plan

If applicable

If you're on a paid subscription plan, please contact your Account Manager about adding all domains to your subscription. Note that additional charges may apply.

Enable account permissions

Turn on account management permissions so our support team can access your profiles and process your request.

Disconnect all integrations

If applicable

If you use any Trustpilot integrations, disconnect them before submitting. You can reconnect them once the merge is complete.
